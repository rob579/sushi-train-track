Privacy Policy

Sushi Train Track · Last updated 3 October 2026

Sushi Train Track helps you add up the plates you take at a sushi train restaurant. This page explains what happens to your information. The short version: the app does not collect your personal information.

What you enter stays on your device

Restaurant names, plate colours and prices, saved menu items and your bills are stored only on your own phone or browser. They are never sent to the developer or to any other company. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. The font it uses is bundled inside the app, so it does not load anything from other companies' websites.

Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Premium. The developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details.

How the app is delivered

The app's files are loaded from sushi.robsofficetips.com, which is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes standard technical information, such as your IP address, in order to deliver those files. The developer does not use this information to identify you. After the first visit the app keeps its own copy and also works offline.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app. Nothing is kept on a server.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
